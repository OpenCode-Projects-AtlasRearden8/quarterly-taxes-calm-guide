# Worksheet: Due-Date Calendar

> **Education only.** Due dates shift when they fall on weekends and
> holidays, deadlines for forms and payments change from year to year, and
> your state runs its own calendar with its own rules. This page teaches the
> *rhythm* and gives you boxes to fill with the **official dates you verify
> for your actual tax year**, straight from the IRS website and your state
> tax agency website. Ten minutes of looking beats trusting a stranger's
> printed date, including this guide's memory of one.

## Part 1: Understand the rhythm (no numbers to memorize)

The pay-as-you-go system (see the [rhythm chapter](../01-estimated-tax-rhythm.md))
generally wants self-employed folks to make **four** estimated payments a
year, each covering income from a rough window of the year:

| Payment | The income window it roughly covers | The usual *pattern* of the date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | roughly January through March | around mid-April |
| Q2 | roughly April through May | around mid-June |
| Q3 | roughly June through August | around mid-September |
| Q4 | roughly September through December | around mid-January of the *next* year |

Notice that the windows do not match calendar quarters, the date amounts and
patterns are the IRS's business and not a promise, and **each year's actual
dates live at irs.gov and your state's tax site**. Fill the next part with
those actual dates.
